For JS8

Faster messages. Longer reach. Less waiting.

Subspace speaks plain JS8 to everyone, and adds a faster mode and smarter tools for when you meet another Subspace station.

Subspace mode, 64 WPM

Four times JS8 Normal, good to about -16 dB. A fifth speed beside Slow, Normal, Fast and Turbo.

No clock to keep right

Subspace mode syncs to the signal itself, not to clock time, so clock drift never stops a decode.

tribbleNet mesh

When the band won't reach, auto-route finds a relay path through stations it has heard, or you pick the hops yourself on the map. Any JS8 program can relay.

Reliable delivery

Messages, files, web links and ICS-213 forms arrive complete, with automatic repeats until every part is confirmed.

Hailing and Typing...

Announce yourself in under 4 seconds with no flood of replies, and let the other station know a reply is on its way.

Spots, live

A built-in map of who hears you, updated every few seconds, with PSKReporter spots that are not rate-limited.

For JTTY

JTTY conversations, the easy way.

JTTY is a keyboard-to-keyboard mode for real conversations. Subspace makes it simple to find people and talk with them.

  • Hears the whole waterfall. Subspace decodes every offset, full-time. WSJT-X decodes JTTY only from 1200 to 1800 Hz unless you move it.
  • No WSJT-X 80-character limit. Just keep typing, and send long messages easily.
  • Text appears as it arrives. Each message grows on screen piece by piece, so you can read along.
  • Click once to address. Pick a station and every message goes to it until you deselect.
  • Every station in its own window. Station Monitor shows one conversation at a time, with no noise from the rest of the band.
  • Logs the way LoTW expects. QSOs are logged as MFSK / JTTY, matching WSJT-X.

New to JTTY?

A step-by-step guide takes you through a complete QSO, click by click. Tip: call CQ between 1200 and 1800 Hz to reach stations running WSJT-X, then move to a quiet spot together.

Spots Map

Who hears you, and the way through.

Every station that heard you, colored by signal strength, with lines to each one. Add PSKReporter spots, choose 5 to 60 minutes, pan and zoom.

  • Auto-route: when a station is out of reach, Subspace finds a relay path through stations it has heard and sends your message along it, hop by hop. Shown here at 30x speed.
  • Manual route: point and click stations on the map to build a relay path with any number of hops.
  • Start a QSO by clicking a station on the map.
Labeled waterfall

Every signal, named.

Call signs are written right on the waterfall, color coded, at the offset and moment each station was heard. Click a label to start a QSO.

Band Activity

One line per station.

Band activity organized by call sign instead of by frequency, so a busy band reads like a list of people. Calls to you are highlighted, and finding a call sign is one search away.

Station Monitor

One conversation, nothing else.

Open a window for any station and follow just that conversation, in both directions, with your own messages shaded. Open as many as you like.

Remote
